Our quality nurse can check about a third of yesterday's charts. The rest go to billing unreviewed.

I built a prototype for family medicine groups that hit this every morning.

Every chart from recent visits gets a first-pass review. Documentation gaps are flagged with a plain reason: unsigned note, diagnosis not supported by the note, missing vitals, medication list conflicts.

In the demo, a note says lisinopril was stopped for a dry cough, but the medication list still shows it with a 90-day refill queued. The chart is flagged the next morning, with both lines side by side and a live countdown to the correction deadline.

The nurse reviews the proposed fix and clicks approve. The chart doesn't change and nothing goes to billing before that click.
